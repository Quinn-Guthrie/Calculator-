## 📊 What It Calculates

### The Big Picture

```
Recipe Savings (sugar vs milk)
MINUS
Solution Costs (SMARTYS vs current)
EQUALS
Net Customer Benefit
```

### 10 Calculation Steps

1. Sugar Removed
2. Sugar Savings
3. Additional Milk
4. Added Milk Cost
5. Recipe Net Saving
6. Current Culture Cost
7. SMARTYS Culture Cost
8. Incremental Culture Cost
9. Additional Solution Cost
10. **Net Benefit** ← This is the key number!
