## Overview

The SMARTYS CIU Calculator helps you quantify the economic benefit of SMARTYS cultures compared to your current solution. It demonstrates two key value propositions:

1. **Sugar Reduction Benefit**: Calculate savings from reducing sugar while maintaining product volume
2. **Solution Comparison**: Compare total economics of SMARTYS vs. current cultures

### Key Features
- ✅ Real-time calculations as you type
- ✅ No login or account required
- ✅ Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- ✅ Dark mode support
- ✅ Save scenarios with presets

## Input Sections

### 1. Scenario Information
Define your production scenario and batch details.

**Scenario Name**
- Example: "Sweet Stirred Yogurt"
- Purpose: Identify your calculation for future reference

**Batch Volume (L)**
- Example: 10,000
- Purpose: Single production run size
- Used for: Per-batch benefit calculation

**Annual Volume (L)**
- Example: 18,000,000
- Purpose: Yearly production volume
- Used for: Annual benefit projection

**Product Density (kg/L)**
- Example: 1.0
- Purpose: Product density
- Typical: 1.0 for yogurt products

---

### 2. Raw Material Costs
Set current market prices for milk and sugar.

**Milk Cost (€/L)**
- Example: 0.20
- Range: Typically 0.15 - 0.40
- Impact: Higher milk costs reduce net benefit

**Sugar Cost (€/kg)**
- Example: 0.60
- Range: Typically 0.40 - 0.80
- Impact: Higher sugar costs increase savings

💡 **Tip**: Update these values quarterly to reflect market changes.

---

### 3. Culture Inputs

#### Current Culture
Your existing culture solution.

**Culture Cost (€/500 DCU)**
- Example: 30
- Purpose: Cost per 500 DCU package

**Culture Dose (DCU/100L)**
- Example: 20
- Purpose: Dosage rate per 100L
- Note: This dose applies to both cultures

#### SMARTYS Culture
The IFF advanced solution (highlighted in blue).

**Culture Cost (€/500 DCU)**
- Example: 40
- Purpose: SMARTYS cost per 500 DCU package

**Culture Dose (DCU/100L)**
- Example: 20
- Purpose: Same dosage rate
- Note: SMARTYS often uses the same dose as current culture

💡 **Tip**: Contact your IFF representative for accurate SMARTYS pricing.

---

### 4. Additional Cost Components (Optional)
Click to expand this section if you use enzymes or sweet modulators.

**Enzyme Cost (€/100L)**
- Default: 0
- When to use: If your recipe includes enzyme treatments
- Example: 0.15

**Sweet Modulator Cost (€/100L)**
- Default: 0
- When to use: If using sweet taste modulators
- Example: 0.25

**Status Indicator**
- Shows "Active" when costs are entered
- Displays total additional cost

💡 **Tip**: Leave at zero if not applicable to your recipe.

---

### 5. Recipe Configuration
Define your sugar reduction targets.

**Current Sugar (%)**
- Example: 7.7
- Purpose: Starting sugar percentage

**New Sugar (%)**
- Example: 5.7
- Purpose: Target reduced sugar level

**Visual Indicator**
- ✅ Green pill shows reduction amount (e.g., "2.0% reduction")
- ⚠️ Warning if new sugar ≥ current sugar

**Milk Replacement Factor**
- Example: 1.0
- Purpose: Liters of milk per kg of sugar removed
- Standard: 1.0 (1:1 replacement)
- Adjustable: Based on your recipe requirements

💡 **Tip**: Start with 1.0 and adjust based on product testing.

## Understanding Results

### KPI Cards (6 Cards)

#### 1. Sugar Removed
**Value**: kg per 100L
**Color**: Neutral (slate)
**Meaning**: Amount of sugar reduced in recipe

#### 2. Sugar Savings
**Value**: € per 100L
**Color**: Green (positive)
**Icon**: Trending up
**Meaning**: Cost savings from reduced sugar

#### 3. Added Milk Cost
**Value**: € per 100L
**Color**: Red (cost)
**Icon**: Plus sign
**Meaning**: Cost of additional milk to replace sugar

#### 4. Recipe Net Saving
**Value**: € per 100L
**Color**: Green/Red (conditional)
**Formula**: Sugar Savings - Added Milk Cost
**Meaning**: Net benefit from recipe optimization alone

#### 5. Incremental Solution Cost
**Value**: € per 100L
**Color**: Orange
**Formula**: SMARTYS cost - Current cost + Additional costs
**Meaning**: Extra cost of SMARTYS solution

#### 6. Net Customer Benefit ⭐ PRIMARY KPI
**Value**: € per 100L
**Color**: Green/Red (conditional)
**Size**: Largest card with bold border
**Formula**: Recipe Net Saving - Incremental Solution Cost
**Meaning**: **Final customer benefit - This is your key number!**

### How to Read Results

**Positive Net Benefit** (Green)
- SMARTYS delivers cost savings
- Recipe optimization exceeds solution cost
- Strong business case for SMARTYS

**Negative Net Benefit** (Red)
- Additional costs exceed recipe savings
- May still be viable for:
  - Marketing (sugar reduction claim)
  - Regulatory compliance
  - Product differentiation

---

### Business Impact Cards

#### Per Batch Impact
**Icon**: Package
**Color**: Blue gradient
**Value**: Net benefit × (Batch Volume / 100)
**Example**: €0.40/100L × 100 batches = €40 per batch
**Use case**: Single production run economics

#### Annual Impact
**Icon**: Calendar
**Color**: Green gradient
**Value**: Net benefit × (Annual Volume / 100)
**Example**: €0.40/100L × 180,000 batches = €72,000 per year
**Use case**: Business case justification

💡 **Tip**: Use annual impact for ROI discussions with management.

## Tips & Best Practices

### Accurate Inputs
✅ **Do**:
- Use actual market prices updated quarterly
- Verify culture costs with your supplier
- Base volumes on actual production data
- Test milk replacement ratios in lab

❌ **Don't**:
- Use outdated pricing
- Guess at culture costs
- Estimate volumes without data
- Assume 1:1 replacement without testing

### Interpreting Results

**When Net Benefit is Close to Zero** (-€0.10 to +€0.10)
- Consider non-economic benefits
- Factor in marketing value of sugar reduction
- Evaluate consumer preference
- Assess competitive positioning

**When Net Benefit is Strongly Positive** (>€0.50/100L)
- Strong case for immediate adoption
- Calculate payback period
- Plan production transition
- Prepare customer communication

**When Net Benefit is Negative** (<-€0.20/100L)
- Review recipe optimization opportunities
- Check for errors in inputs
- Discuss with IFF technical team
- Evaluate alternative scenarios

### Scenario Planning

**Create Multiple Scenarios**:
1. **Baseline**: Current market conditions
2. **Optimistic**: Lower milk prices
3. **Conservative**: Higher SMARTYS costs
4. **Best Case**: Maximum sugar reduction

**Compare Side-by-Side**:
- Screenshot or note each scenario
- Create simple comparison table
- Share with stakeholders
- Make data-driven decision

### Presentation Tips

**To Management**:
- Lead with Annual Impact
- Show positive ROI
- Compare to other cost reduction initiatives
- Highlight non-financial benefits

**To Technical Teams**:
- Show per 100L calculations
- Explain recipe optimization
- Discuss implementation steps
- Address technical questions

**To Sales/Marketing**:
- Emphasize sugar reduction %
- Show consumer appeal
- Discuss positioning vs. competitors
- Provide clean claim language

## Glossary

**DCU**: Dose Culture Unit - Standard measurement for culture dosage
**CIU**: Cost-In-Use - Total economic cost including all factors
**SMARTYS**: IFF's advanced culture solution for sugar reduction
**Milk Replacement Factor**: Ratio of milk added per kg sugar removed
**Net Benefit**: Final economic benefit after all costs
**Per Batch**: Benefit for single production run
**Annual Impact**: Projected benefit over full year
